# User Guide

Register with a unique username and strong password; no email address is required. Log in to open the setups that belong only to your account. Create as many setups as needed, then choose technologies, keywords, sources, and a date range. Select Scan Now to search with those choices. If you change the scope, select Scan Now again to replace the previous results with a fresh search. Values typed under Your additions become removable chips. Scan for Zero Days uses its own separate focused-results section. Use table filters without rescanning. Open Review to inspect a threat and ask grounded questions. Use a row's Email button for one threat, select several checkboxes and choose Email selected, or choose Email all found for the complete filtered result set. The email form offers editable subject and message options. Export Excel downloads exactly the currently filtered result set. Use Log out at the bottom of the menu when finished on a shared computer.
